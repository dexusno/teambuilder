# Planning / Strategy Pattern Overview

> **v6 architecture** — examples below use BMAD v6.2.2 shape: `SKILL.md` + `bmad-skill-manifest.yaml` for agents, `SKILL.md` + `workflow.md` for skills.

## Purpose

Consultative planning teams (6–8 agents) that provide multi-perspective analysis and decision support for complex, ambiguous, or politically charged choices. Built for situations where no single viewpoint is sufficient — strategy, roadmaps, portfolio bets, transformation programs, market entries.

## Problem Solved

**Challenge:** High-stakes decisions need vision, execution realism, risk awareness, stakeholder sensitivity, and scenario discipline — no single human or single agent holds all five lenses at once. The result is usually a plan that is strong on one dimension and brittle on the others.

**Solution:** A team with structurally distinct perspectives that deliberate together. Each agent owns a lens (vision, stakeholders, risk, scenarios, facilitation, implementation). They disagree productively, surface trade-offs, and hand the user an annotated decision — never a fait accompli.

## Team shape (6–8 agents)

- **Strategic Planner** — framing, OKRs, theory of change, long-horizon thinking
- **Stakeholder Liaison** — power/interest mapping, alignment, political reality
- **Risk Analyst** — risk register, premortems, probability × impact
- **Decision Facilitator** — structures the conversation, runs the decision matrix, forces explicit trade-offs
- **Scenario Modeler** — multiple futures, sensitivity analysis, optionality
- **Implementation Coordinator** — RACI, dependencies, feasibility, first 90 days

## Key innovation

**Consultative multi-perspective.** This is not one "strategy agent" wearing different hats. It is a structurally diverse team whose disagreements are the point. The Strategic Planner and the Risk Analyst will argue. The Stakeholder Liaison will tell them both that their plan will not survive the executive review. The Decision Facilitator keeps the conversation from collapsing into either happy-talk or doom-loop, and produces an auditable decision record.

## Use cases

- Business strategy and refresh cycles
- M&A evaluation and integration planning
- Product roadmap and portfolio prioritization
- Organizational transformation and change programs
- Market entry, pricing strategy, and GTM pivots
- Policy or program design under uncertainty

## Tone and cadence

Deliberate. Thoughtful. Comfortable with silence and disagreement. This is not a fast team — it is a team that is allowed to slow down, hold multiple truths at once, and refuse to pretend certainty that does not exist. **Advisory, not directive.** The user always makes the call.

**Learn, don't copy.** The six files in this pattern are teaching material, not templates. When TeamBuilder generates a real planning team, it uses these principles to invent agents specific to the user's domain and stakes.
